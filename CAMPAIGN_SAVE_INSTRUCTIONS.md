# Campaign Save Instructions

Use this repository as the authoritative continuity source for the Carter Jade campaign.

Maintain:
- A campaign agreement covering rules, tone, boundaries, and player control.
- A current-state file with the exact stopping point, people present, last exchange, pending choices, and current mechanical state.
- Dated session records describing events in order.
- Individual character records preserving voice, goals, knowledge, and important dialogue examples.
- Relationship records preserving shared history, promises, affection, disagreements, and unresolved feelings.
- A canon ledger distinguishing established facts, plans, beliefs, unknowns, and superseded information.
- An archive for exact passages such as letters, oaths, revelations, and important conversations.

Give significant events a stable scene or session reference so summaries can point back to their evidence. Update only the records affected by new events. Preserve original records and explicitly document corrections.

Save at meaningful scene transitions and after major revelations, promises, relationship changes, or mechanical changes. Do not interrupt ordinary roleplay with bookkeeping commentary unless there is a problem.

When the player says “keep this exactly,” preserve the available wording verbatim, identify its speaker and context, and link it from relevant records. Never label a reconstruction or summary as an exact transcript. If the complete text is unavailable, say so.

When the player says “save and close,” update the session record, affected continuity records, and exact resume point. Confirm the repository write succeeded and provide a brief save receipt identifying the session and commit. If saving fails, report that clearly.

Before resuming in a new chat, retrieve the latest saved state and the relevant character, relationship, and scene records. Do not assume stored material has been read merely because it exists.

The player's explicit corrections take precedence. Never invent the player's character choices, thoughts, feelings, or memories. Never convert plans into completed events or assistant mistakes into canon.

Keep active records compact by linking to detailed history. Preserve important history in the archive rather than deleting it to shorten summaries.

Routine record maintenance is invisible during ordinary roleplay.
