# Session CJ-2026-10-11 — Day 9–11 Leatherwork and the Ridge Beast

**Status:** Closed at the tanner's yard, late morning on Day 11.  
**Stable scene reference:** `CJ-2026-10-11-RIDGE-BEAST-AND-TANNER`

## Session summary

### Day 9 — Market and commission
- Carter worked his permanent stall in Bellweather's eastern market row while waiting for the unnamed caravan laborer who commissioned custom work gloves.
- The woman arrived late, explaining a wagon wheel had gone bad. Carter delivered the finished gloves. She checked the fit and movement, tested a knot with rope, and expressed satisfaction. She paid the remaining 2 gp; the 4 gp commission is complete. Her name remains unknown.
- Carter set up his homestead inquiry sign: “CARTER JADE LEATHERWORKS — Custom Orders & Repairs — Please Inquire at the House.”
- Carter returned home and began the Captain's Coat from the preserved unusual canine hide, using Merrit's paper pattern. He inspected and mapped the hide, adjusted the pattern to avoid a pocket/fold conflict and a reinforcement edge crossing a bend, then cut the major panels. No assembly yet at this stage.

### Day 9 late afternoon/evening — Repair commission
- Carter returned to the stall. An unnamed broad-shouldered working man asked about a splitting knife sheath. Carter inspected it with the knife removed: the sheath body remained serviceable, the belt attachment was failing due to stretched stitch holes, and the side seam near the mouth had loosened.
- Carter offered 3 sp for a basic repair, or 9 sp for reinforced backing and belt loop, repaired side seam, strengthened edges, and a snap to secure the handle without obstructing a quick draw.
- The customer chose the 9 sp upgrade. Terms: pay on delivery, promised for the morning of Day 10. Customer left both sheath and knife with Carter.

### Day 10 — Delivery and coat work
- Carter worked what he could on the sheath that evening, fitting a new backing and replacement loop, repairing the side seam, and preparing edge reinforcement. He left final snap placement and testing until morning.
- Before dawn, Carter made coffee and completed the repair. He reinforced the edges, fitted the snap, tug-tested the loop and backing, flexed the sheath, checked the seam, and repeatedly drew/replaced the knife. The snap retained the sheath without obstructing the draw.
- The customer returned as promised, tested the repaired sheath on his belt, approved it, and paid 9 sp. His name remains unknown. Carter did not put his maker's mark on the entire sheath because he did not make the original item.
- Carter resumed the Captain's Coat. He adjusted a shoulder seam that restricted movement, assembled the body and rear split, reinforced the split's upper termination, fitted sleeves and forearm protection, and tried on the partially assembled coat. The shoulder movement was corrected. The collar remains unattached pending adjustment for raised/folded comfort. Pockets, fasteners, tooling, and finishing remain incomplete.
- Late afternoon, Carter walked through town, ate beef stew and bread and drank whiskey at the tavern. The narrated price was 6 cp. Two teamsters mentioned a wagon that lost a wheel near the old stone bridge east of town; they also mentioned something nosing around wagons and deep marks in the ground. No verified identity or attack was established.

### Day 11 — Tracking and kill
- At dawn Carter took his bow, arrows, hunting traps, and old gray mule toward the old stone bridge. He followed broad, clawed four-footed tracks into the woods. Smaller canine-like tracks were also present, some turning back toward the road. There was no blood or abandoned cargo at the wagon rut.
- Carter followed the large tracks for about twenty minutes. He found a shallow drainage ditch, a stronger print with four toes and claws, a faint musky odor, snapped branches, disturbed earth, and a partly eaten deer carcass. Tracks continued toward a low ridge.
- Carter named his mule **Baity**. He took Baity into a clearing and climbed a nearby oak to observe the trail. He cast Hunter's Mark on a large, dark, bear-like but oddly proportioned four-legged creature, about forty yards away, broad in the forequarters with coarse dark fur, blunt muzzle, and long claws.
- Carter shot from the tree: first attack 22 to hit for 14 damage; second shot was declared a critical hit for 23 damage; third attack 19 to hit for 9 damage. Total damage was 46. The creature collapsed and was confirmed dead, with no breathing or movement.
- Carter climbed down, praised Baity, retrieved all three arrows (two serviceable, one with a slightly bent shaft requiring attention), then skinned the creature. The hide was recovered as one large, mostly continuous hide with three localized arrow-damage areas. The carcass and usable claws/teeth were left at the clearing; claws/teeth were only offered as possible trophies, not confirmed collected.
- Carter secured the raw hide on Baity and took it to Oren Vale, the tanner at the cooper's yard. Oren inspected it and accepted it for trade credit, recording the unusual origin and three arrow wounds. **Final credit value is pending cleaning/tanning assessment; no credit amount has yet been posted.** The hide is no longer at Carter's home and is now in Oren's care.
